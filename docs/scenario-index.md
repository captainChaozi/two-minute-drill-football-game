Beyond these pages, the home page offers **Today's Real Ending** (a recent completed game), 20 archived 2023 snapshots and 6 original training drills.

How to read a scenario page:

1. Note score, clock, ball position, down and distance, and **both teams' timeouts**.
2. Read the "reads" section; it lists the trade-offs, not the answer.
3. Play it, then read the decision review. It describes choices; it is not a perfect-play score.
